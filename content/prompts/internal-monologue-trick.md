Before responding, think through the question in <thinking> tags.
Then provide your final answer outside the tags.
The user will only see what's outside the <thinking> tags.

Example:
<thinking>
The user is asking about X. Key considerations: A, B, C.
The best answer is...
</thinking>
[Final answer here]
