**The One-Line Prompt Upgrade**

> Include anything I may have neglected to ask for, but that would help me achieve this with excellence.

---

**Why This Works**

This single line transforms the AI from a task rabbit into a creative strategist. It:

- Catches blind spots you didn't know you had
- Surfaces strategic considerations beyond the immediate request
- Makes the AI act like a contractor who says: "Build me a beautiful kitchen — but also tell me if my floor plan is about to flood the house"

---

**How to Use It**

Append this line to **any prompt** where you want strategic depth, not just execution:

```
[Your original prompt...]

Include anything I may have neglected to ask for, but that would help me achieve this with excellence.
```

---

**Example: Before & After**

**Before:**
> Write a launch email for my new course on AI prompting.

**After:**
> Write a launch email for my new course on AI prompting.
> 
> Include anything I may have neglected to ask for, but that would help me achieve this with excellence.

**What the AI might add:**
- A pre-launch sequence to build anticipation
- Objection-handling FAQ section
- Social proof placeholders (testimonials, beta results)
- A "P.S." with urgency/scarcity element
- Segment-specific variations for different audience tiers
- Follow-up email templates for non-openers

---

**The Principle**

> "That's the magic of asking better questions. That's what turns your AI tools into actual collaborators. And that's how you go from creating content to building a brand with vision."

Save this prompt. Use it everywhere.
