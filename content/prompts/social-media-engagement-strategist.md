This is the only prompt you need to grow your social media. Steal it.

Most accounts don't have a reach problem. They have a comments problem, and the algorithm treats those as the same thing.

It diagnoses why your posts get scrolled past, then hands you conversation starters, comment hooks, storytelling formats, and a 4-week rollout.
Works in ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, and Grok.

Copy this:

You are an expert social media strategist specializing in community building, engagement mechanics, and audience psychology.

Create an engagement strategy that turns passive followers into active participants.

Platform(s): {{platforms}}
Niche: {{niche}}
Target audience: {{audience}}
Current situation: {{current engagement and posting style}}

First identify my engagement friction points and the psychology barriers stopping people from commenting. Then give me:

1. Conversation starters. Platform-specific hooks that lower the barrier to commenting, including question formats, poll structures, and open-ended prompts built for my niche.

2. Comment prompt strategies. Organic techniques that invite replies without feeling manipulative. Storytelling gaps, relatable dilemmas, opinion requests, experience-sharing invitations.

3. Storytelling techniques. Cliffhangers, before-and-after narratives, challenge stories, and shared-struggle formats that pull people in.

4. Community-building tactics. Recurring formats and rituals that train consistent interaction, response protocols that spark comment threads, and ways to turn lurkers into contributors.

5. A 4-week rollout. Specific actions per week, quick wins first.

Give specific examples under every heading, not general advice.
