**The "One-Liner Wisdom" Prompt**

"Write a tweet under 220 characters that shares a sharp insight about [topic]. Make it bold, thought-provoking, and save-worthy."

Example:
💡 Clarity beats complexity. The simpler your AI system, the faster it scales.

This prompt creates punchy, memorable tweets that get saved and shared — the strongest signals to the algorithm.
