Don't answer my question yet. First do this:

1. Tell me what assumptions I'm making...
2. Tell me what information would significantly change your answer...
3. Tell me the most common mistake people make...
4. Then ask me the one question that would make your answer actually useful...

Only after I answer – give me the output.
