Five prompts designed to shatter mental loops and generate breakthrough ideas when you're creatively stuck. From Forbes contributor Jodie Cook on AI and founder-led growth.

## 1. The Taboo Pain Points Prompt
> "List the top 10 taboo pain points in [your industry] that no one talks about publicly. Then show me how addressing even one of these could differentiate my business completely."

Boring ideas follow all the rules. Revolutionary ones dance on the edge.

## 2. The "Get Sued" Prompt
> "Design a product or service so outrageous it would get you sued. Then work backwards: strip the questionable bits while preserving the excitement. That spark making lawyers nervous is your biggest opportunity. Find the stuff so groundbreaking it should be illegal."

## 3. The Groundhog Day Breaker
> "My current thinking on [topic] is: [describe your usual approach]. Give me 5 completely different angles of attack that would make my current approach look primitive. Attack my mental loops from unexpected angles."

Your groundhog day thinking is your business trap.

## 4. The Unspoken Truths Miner
> "What are the unspoken truths in [your industry] that everyone knows but no one says? List them brutally. Then show me how to build a business model around the most uncomfortable one."

Mine the unspoken truths.

## 5. The Nervous System Reset
> "I'm feeling stuck on [specific challenge]. Before giving me ideas, first give me a 3-step protocol to reset my nervous system for breakthrough thinking. Then give me 3 ideas that would only occur to someone in that state."

Reset your nervous system, orchestrate productive conflict, and strip away safe thinking.
