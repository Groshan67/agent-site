A complete 7-step editing workflow using Claude, each prompt targeting a specific aspect of article quality. Run them in sequence for best results.

## 1. Clarity Pass
> "Edit this article for clarity only. Do not add ideas. Remove confusing sentences. Shorten where possible."

Catches long sentences you stopped noticing. Trims mental fog without touching your voice.

## 2. Flow Check
> "Read this like a human reader. Point out where the flow breaks or feels abrupt."

Good at spotting jumps, especially between sections that made sense in your head but not on the page.

## 3. Tone Reality Check
> "Does this sound like a real person or like advice content? Be honest."

Exposes when writing sounds stiff or preachy instead of natural.

## 4. Redundancy Cleaner
> "Find repeated ideas and say which lines can be removed without losing meaning."

Writers repeat themselves when unsure. This cuts that out fast.

## 5. Weak Sentence Detector
> "Highlight sentences that feel generic, safe, or low-impact."

Exposes lines that feel like filler but pretend to be insight.

## 6. Opening Hook Fix
> "Rewrite the opening to make someone want to keep reading. Keep my tone."

Rarely nail the opening on the first try. Sharpens it without turning it clickbait.

## 7. Ending With a Point
> "Does this ending land? Suggest a stronger takeaway without adding fluff."

Most endings fade out instead of closing. This makes sure the article actually says something at the end.
