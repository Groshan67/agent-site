Use the APE framework for every prompt:

- **Action**: What you want done (write, analyze, summarize, fix, plan)
- **Purpose**: Why it matters or who will use the output
- **Expectation**: Specific format, length, or quality requirement

Example: "Write a weekly status email (Action) for my manager to review project progress (Purpose) as a 5-bullet summary under 150 words (Expectation)."
