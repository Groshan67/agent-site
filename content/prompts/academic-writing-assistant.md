A structured process for using Claude/ChatGPT as an academic writing assistant that keeps the writing authentically yours. The core principle: **you write every sentence; the AI only suggests directions.**

---

### Setup

**Initial prompt:**
> "I would like you to act as my academic writing assistant. You don't have to write for me. I will write myself but I'd need your help. Does that make sense?"

The AI will confirm it can help with brainstorming, structuring, proofreading, etc.

---

### Step 1: Get Starting Suggestions

**Prompt:**
> "I am working on a journal article on [your topic]. Give me five suggestions about how I should start my paper."

Read through the suggestions and pick the one you find most interesting.

---

### Step 2: Write Your First Sentence

Open a new document and write a single sentence based on the chosen suggestion. Don't try to write a "perfect" sentence — just write the first thing that comes to mind.

---

### Step 3: Get Directions for the Next Sentence

**Prompt:**
> "I followed your suggestion number [N] and wrote the following sentence:
> [Sentence that you just wrote]
> Please give me five directions in which I can write the next sentence. Do not give me any sentences."

**Critical rule:** Tell the AI not to give you any sentences of its own. If you use a sentence generated by the AI, it will no longer remain your own work and could be flagged as AI-generated.

---

### Step 4: Repeat

Pick one of the five directions, write the next sentence yourself, then ask for five new directions:

> "I followed your suggestion number [N] and wrote:
> [Your new sentence]
> Please give me five directions in which I can write the next sentence. Do not give me any sentences."

After repeating this process a few times, you'll have written a decent paragraph.

---

### Step 5: Transition to Next Paragraph

Once you have a complete paragraph:

**Prompt:**
> "I have followed your suggestions and now I have the following paragraph:
> [paste your paragraph]
> Please give me five directions in which I should start my next paragraph. Please keep in mind coherence, cohesion, and a smooth transition."

---

### Step 6: Continue Until Target Length

Repeat the process until you reach your target word count (most journal articles are 5,000–8,000 words).

---

### Key Principles

1. **Never ask the AI to write sentences for you** — this corrupts the process and risks plagiarism/AI-detection flags.

2. **You remain the author** — the AI acts as a thinking partner, suggesting directions, questions, and structural guidance.

3. **Works with any version** — free or paid versions of ChatGPT or Claude both work. The original thread used Claude 3 Opus.

4. **Iterative and low-pressure** — one sentence at a time removes writer's block and keeps momentum.

---

### Why This Works

- **Authenticity guaranteed:** Every word is yours
- **Overcomes blank-page paralysis:** You only need to write one sentence at a time
- **Maintains flow:** AI suggestions keep you moving without taking over
- **Structural coherence:** Paragraph-level transitions ensure logical progression
- **Scalable:** Works for anything from an abstract to a full dissertation
