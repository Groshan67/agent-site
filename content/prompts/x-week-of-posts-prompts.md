**10 Copy-Paste Prompts for X (Twitter) Content**

---

**1. Build-in-Public Posts**

> You're a solo founder building [product] for [audience]. Write 5 build-in-public posts about [this week's progress]. Each under 280 chars, conversational, no hashtags, end with a question.

---

**2. Hook-Only Tweet**

> Write a hook-only tweet about [topic]. Make it a bold, counterintuitive one-liner that stops the scroll. No fluff, no explanation — just the hook.

---

**3. Three Angles from One Idea**

> Turn this idea — [paste your rough thought] — into 3 different tweet angles: one story, one list, one hot take.

---

**4. "What I Wish I Knew" Tweet**

> Write a 'what I wish I knew before' tweet for someone just starting [niche/tool/process]. Max 240 chars.

---

**5. Helpful Replies to Pain Points**

> Write 3 replies to someone who just posted about [common pain point in your niche]. Sound helpful, not salesy.

---

**6. Strip to the Most Shareable Sentence**

> Take this long post — [paste it] — and strip it to the single most shareable sentence.

---

**7. Thread Opener**

> Write a thread opener for [topic]. First tweet under 200 chars. Should feel incomplete enough that people tap to read more.

---

**8. Problem-First Product Post**

> Write a product post for [product] aimed at [audience]. Lead with their problem, not your features. No hype.

---

**9. Educational "Did You Know" Tweets**

> Write 5 educational 'did you know' tweets about [your niche]. Plain language, one idea each, under 260 chars.

---

**10. Rewrite Three Ways**

> Rewrite this tweet — [paste] — three ways: shorter, punchier, question format.

---

**Usage Notes**

- These are starting points — always edit AI output before posting
