/skeptic + ULTRATHINK

Most prompts try to improve the answer. This combo improves the QUESTION.

**/skeptic** — challenges the premise, finds hidden assumptions, spots XY problems.  
**ULTRATHINK** — deep reasoning mode, explores the problem space before solving.

---

**Prompt:**

```
/skeptic: Before solving, tell me:
1. What's the REAL problem here (vs. the stated one)?
2. What assumptions am I making that might be wrong?
3. What would a better question look like?

ULTRATHINK: Now explore the problem space deeply:
- Map the decision landscape
- Identify constraints and trade-offs
- Consider second- and third-order effects
- Only then propose a solution
```

Use when: you suspect you're asking the wrong question, or the problem is poorly framed.
