The prompt I run before every stakeholder review that predicts the exact questions execs will ask:

You are a skeptical CFO and CTO reviewing this proposal. What are the 5 hardest questions you will ask? For each question, explain the underlying concern and what evidence would satisfy you. Focus on: ROI assumptions, technical risk, timeline realism, resource constraints, and opportunity cost.
